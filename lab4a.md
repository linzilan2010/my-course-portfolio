## Exercise 2: Recipe Card

### Fried Rice

#### Ingredients
- Rice
- Eggs
- Green onions
- Soy sauce
- Cooking oil
- Any meat you want add in

#### Cooking Steps

1. Heat the cooking oil in a pan.
2. Add the eggs and cook them.
3. Add the rice and mix well
4. Add the meat you want
5. Add soy sauce and green onions.
6. Stir everything together and serve.

**Tip: Using leftover rice can make the fried rice taste better.**
